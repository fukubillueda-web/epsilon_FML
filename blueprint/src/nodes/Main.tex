\begin{theorem}[Exported First Main Lemma]\label{mod:main}
Let $K/F$ be a cyclic extension of prime degree.  For every continuous
quasi-character $\chi_F$ of $F^\times$ and every nontrivial continuous
additive character $\psi_F$ of $F$, the canonical local constants satisfy
the First Main identity, with $\chi_K=\chi_F\circ N_{K/F}$,
$\psi_K=\psi_F\circ\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F}$, and the product over the full
group $S(K/F)$ of norm characters.
