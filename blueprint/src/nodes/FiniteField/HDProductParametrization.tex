\begin{theorem}[Hasse-Davenport tuple parametrization]\label{mod:finitefield-hdproductparametrization}
Let $G$ be a commutative group.  For $a\in G$ and $r\geq0$, define the
prescribed-product fiber
\[
 \operatorname{HDProductTupleFiber}(G,r,a)
 =\left\{x:\operatorname{Fin}(r)\longrightarrow G:
          \prod_{i:\operatorname{Fin}(r)}x_i=a\right\}.
\]
For every $n\geq0$ there is an explicit equivalence
\[
 \operatorname{HDProductTupleFiber}(G,n+1,a)
 \simeq (\operatorname{Fin}(n)\longrightarrow G).
\]
It sends a surviving tuple to its first $n$ coordinates.  Its inverse sends
$y:\operatorname{Fin}(n)\to G$ to
\[
 \operatorname{snoc}\!\left(y,
       a\left(\prod_{i:\operatorname{Fin}(n)}y_i\right)^{-1}\right).
\]
Thus the final coordinate is uniquely forced; no representative or
existence choice is suppressed.

If $G$ is finite and $R$ is an additive commutative monoid, every finite sum
over the product fiber is reindexed by this equivalence:
\[
 \sum_{x\in\operatorname{HDProductTupleFiber}(G,n+1,a)}f(x)
 =\sum_{y:\operatorname{Fin}(n)\to G}
   f\!\left(\operatorname{snoc}\!\left(y,
       a\left(\prod_i y_i\right)^{-1}\right)\right).
\]
In particular all fibers of the $(n+1)$-fold product map have the same
cardinality, explicitly
\[
 \left|\operatorname{HDProductTupleFiber}(G,n+1,a)\right|=|G|^n.
\]
Taking $G=k^\times$ gives the surviving-tuple parametrization used after
expanding the Gauss-sum products in the proof of the Hasse--Davenport
multiplication formula.
