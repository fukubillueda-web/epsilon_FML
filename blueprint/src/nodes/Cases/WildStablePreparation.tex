\begin{theorem}[Wild stable preparation]
\label{mod:cases-wildstablepreparation}
Let $K/F$ be a ramified prime-cyclic extension of nonarchimedean local
fields, and let
\[
 P:\texttt{PrimeCyclicPreparation}(F,K)
\]
be its prepared lower break, residue-degree-one proof, and integral
monogenic upper uniformizer.  Assume that $K/F$ is wildly ramified and that
$[K:F]$ is odd.  Let $\Delta_F$ and $\Delta_K$ be local-constant functions
satisfying \texttt{IsDeltaFiniteLocalConstant}, and let $\chi_F$ and
$\psi_F$ be arbitrary exact multiplicative- and additive-conductor data.

Write $\chi_{\min}$ for the least-conductor member selected from the complete
norm-character orbit of $\chi_F$.  If
\[
  P.t+1\le
  \left\lfloor\frac{m_F(\chi_{\min})}{2}\right\rfloor,
\]
then Lean proves the fixed-character First Main identity
\[
 \Delta_K(\chi_F\circ N_{K/F},
          \psi_F\circ\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F})
 \prod_{\mu\in S(K/F)}\Delta_F(\mu,\psi_F)
 =
 \prod_{\mu\in S(K/F)}\Delta_F(\mu\chi_F,\psi_F),
\]
where $S(K/F)=\texttt{NormCharacter}(F,K)$ is made finite from $P$ and the
products include its identity element.  Equivalently, the conclusion is
exactly
\[
 \texttt{FirstMainIdentity}\ F\ K\ \Delta_F\ \Delta_K\
   \chi_F.\texttt{character}\ \psi_F.\texttt{character}.
\]

The proof sets
\[
 d=m_F(\chi_{\min})/2,
 \qquad \varepsilon=m_F(\chi_{\min})\bmod 2,
\]
and constructs the genuine division-with-remainder stationary conductor
decomposition.  Wildness and the displayed stable inequality imply both
$m_F(\chi_{\min})>1$ and the strict high-conductor bound.  The literal norm
pullback is packaged at conductor
\[
 \psi_{P.t,[K:F]}(m_F(\chi_{\min})-1)+1,
\]
using the current Herbrand high-conductor theorem.  The literal trace
pullback is packaged at additive conductor
\[
 [K:F]n_F(\psi_F)+([K:F]-1)(P.t+1).
\]
Its actual conductor is again divided by two to obtain $d_K$ and
$\varepsilon_K$.

Lean chooses an actual admissible downstairs denominator and an admissible
denominator for every norm character.  It then takes a literal preimage of
the downstairs stationary coefficient quotient and keeps its quotient
equality attached to the resulting
\texttt{StationaryClassRepresentative}.  In each parity branch this same
representative is passed through \texttt{highConductorParameter\_stableOdd},
the compatible phases, and the final wild-stable consumer.  Odd degree and
the exact conductor relation give
$\varepsilon_K=\varepsilon$.

For $\varepsilon=0$, both critical coordinates are \texttt{even} and the
residual comparison is literally true.  For $\varepsilon=1$, the lower and
upper critical coordinates are the actual powers of
\texttt{ramifiedHasseLowerUniformizer} and
\texttt{ramifiedHasseUpperUniformizer}.  The complete certificate is exactly
\texttt{ramifiedHassePreparation} applied to the same ordered denominator
pair, high parameter row, and quotient representative.  Thus its
second-order norm precision remains $2d+1$, and the elementary stationary
translation stays coupled to the Hasse phase.

The mapped upstairs denominator and the stable twist denominator are then
passed to \texttt{firstMain\_wildStable}.  In particular the twist direction
is the manuscript's $\mu(\Gamma_F/\beta)$, never its inverse.  The generic
\texttt{firstMainIdentity\_of\_deltaFinite\_product} bridge converts the
completed product to \texttt{FirstMainIdentity} for $\chi_{\min}$.  Finally,
membership of $\chi_{\min}$ in the original complete norm-character orbit
and invariance of both sides under left norm-character multiplication
transport the identity back to $\chi_F$.
