\begin{theorem}[Wild quadratic strict-high coordinate transport]
\label{mod:cases-wildquadratic-highcoordinatetransport}
In the strict-high range, construct the upper and lower-product coordinate
certificates from the original high quadratic parameter record.  The public
producer is \texttt{WildQuadraticHighParameterData.coordinateTransport}; its
receiver is the original \(Q.\texttt{parameters}\), not a reconstructed
transport record.

The construction retains
\(\beta=\delta N(\beta_1)\), \(\alpha=N(\alpha_1)\),
\(c_F=\alpha\delta\), \(u=\beta_1/\alpha_1\), and
\(n=\beta/c_F\).  It proves the strict-high depth identities, the zero and
quadratic residual terms, the actual scalar \(\lambda_\tau=1\), and the
actual extension and twist phase equalities uniformly in \(q\).

Use the original high representatives and retain the factor \(\delta\) in
\(\beta=\delta N(\beta_1)\).  Never replace this identity by
\(\beta=N(\beta_1)\).  Preserve the actual elementary and Hasse translations,
and select no refinement \(q\).
\end{theorem}
