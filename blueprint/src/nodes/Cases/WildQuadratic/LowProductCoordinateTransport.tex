\begin{theorem}[Wild quadratic low lower-product coordinate transport]
\label{mod:cases-wildquadratic-lowproductcoordinatetransport}
Using the certified low upper/boundary package, construct the independent
lower-product coordinate package in the strict-low and odd-boundary rows.
The boundary certificate consumes the selected-source residue already proved
by the upper package, preventing a duplicate or norm-only reconstruction of
\(\rho\).

The output contains the \(q\)-polymorphic actual product certificate and the
actual norm, base, and twist phase-source equalities.  It selects no
characteristic-two refinement.

universally quantified.
\end{theorem}
