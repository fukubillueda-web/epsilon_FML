\begin{theorem}[Wild odd affine-translation bridge]
\label{mod:cases-wildodd-affinetranslationbridge}
For an odd representative-change datum $R$, write
$c_0=\beta'_R-\beta_R$ for its exact source-to-selected representative
difference.  If
\[
 c_0\in\mathfrak p_E^{d_R+1},
\]
then its residual translation coefficient is zero:
\[
 \Delta B_R=0.
\]
Indeed, $\beta_R$ has order zero and the critical coordinate has order
$d_R$, so $c_0/(\beta_R\delta_R)$ lies in the maximal ideal.  Its residue
translation parameter is therefore zero, and the exact identity
$\Delta B_R=A_Ra_R$ gives the result.  In particular, the proof retains
$c_0=\beta'_R-\beta_R$, the source representative in the denominator, and
the chosen critical coordinate.

For every nonzero High index $j$, take the actual datum
\texttt{highTeichmullerLiteralRepresentativeChange}.  Its difference is
exactly $[j]-j_{\mathbf Z}$.  Since $p$ and $T=t+1$ are odd, the successor
half-depth theorem gives
\[
 [j]-j_{\mathbf Z}\in
 \mathfrak p_F^{\lfloor T/2\rfloor+1}.
\]
Thus $\Delta_j^H=0$.  Applying the exact complete-function transport before
simplifying its translation proves that the product of the actual High
critical factors equals the clean product of the literal natural-power rows
used by the scalar-pencil calculation.

For the Low row the selected-minus-source difference is
\[
 ([j]-j_{\mathbf Z})\alpha.
\]
The same successor congruence, multiplied by the actual supplied unit
$\alpha$, puts this difference in
$\mathfrak p_F^{\lfloor T/2\rfloor+1}$.  The positive source-to-selected
translations exposed separately by the actual norm-row and boundary-twist
row theorems therefore satisfy
\[
 \Delta_j^R=0,\qquad \Delta_j^T=0.
\]
Only after these two vanishings are invoked does the translated boundary
critical quotient simplify to the untranslated quotient consumed by the
boundary pencil.  No cancellation theorem for arbitrary nonzero
translations is used.  Elementary stationary factors, quotient classes,
raw inverse norm factors, and denominator orientations are unchanged.
