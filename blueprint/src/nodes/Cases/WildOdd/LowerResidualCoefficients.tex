\begin{lemma}[Wild odd lower residual coefficients]
\label{mod:cases-wildodd-lowerresidualcoefficients}
Let $(A,B)$ denote respectively the polar and affine coefficients extracted
from one complete Lamprecht critical function.  The common lower coordinate
is the one supplied by PhaseReduction: it is the norm of the common upper
coordinate.  For a source-to-selected coordinate scalar $\lambda$, the
transport identities used here have the fixed direction
\[
 \Phi_{\mathrm{sel}}(x)=\Phi_{\mathrm{src}}(\lambda x),\qquad
 A_{\mathrm{sel}}=\lambda^2A_{\mathrm{src}},\qquad
 B_{\mathrm{sel}}=\lambda B_{\mathrm{src}}.
\]

Suppose first that the post-drop conductor $t+1$ is odd.  Normalize the
literal norm-generator row as $(\eta _0,\eta _0\gamma _0)$.  For
$j\ne0$, represented in Lean by the subtype
\texttt{OddNormIndex}, the literal natural-multiple row is
\[
 (j\eta _0,j\eta _0\gamma _0).
\]
The table's actual Teichmuller representative is the selected row, whereas
the literal natural multiple is the source row.  If $\Delta_j$ is the exact
translation coefficient furnished by
\texttt{OddRepresentativeChangeData}, its actual pair is therefore
\[
 (j\eta _0,j\eta _0\gamma _0+\Delta_j).
\]
The sign is positive.  The strict-lower twist row is proved equal to this
actual norm row.  The exceptional $j=0$ row is handled separately and, when
its conductor is odd, is normalized as $(\eta,\eta\gamma)$.

At the boundary put $d_0=\eta/\eta _0$; the denominator $\eta _0$ is proved
nonzero before this quotient is formed.  The zero row is the clean
specialization of the boundary formula and is also the actual base row.  For
every nonzero index the selected boundary row is
\[
 \left(
   \eta _0(j+d_0),
   \eta _0(j\gamma _0+d_0\gamma)+\Delta_j
 \right).
\]
In addition, $j+d_0\ne0$ is proved from the nonvanishing polar coefficient of
the actual selected twist row before any later use as a denominator.  In the
strict-upper (above-break) regime every index, including zero, has the actual
base pair $(\eta,\eta\gamma)$.  In each corresponding even-conductor branch
the complete function is one and the pair is $(0,0)$.  The high literal-to-
actual Teichmuller representative change is also recorded with
$B_{\mathrm{actual}}=B_{\mathrm{literal}}+\Delta_j$.

For the source-tied correction units, Lean first records
$N_{K/F}(u)=n$, the separate numerator and denominator nonvanishing proofs
for $z_0$ and for every $j\ne0$, and the nonvanishing of $[j]$ before forming
$(u+[j])/[j]$.  It then proves
\[
 z_j=\frac{N_{K/F}(u+[j])}{n+[j]},\qquad
 z_0=\frac{N_{K/F}(n-u)}{\prod_k(n+[k])}.
\]
Before applying a character, the selected representative ratios retain the
inverse norm factors exactly:
\[
 \frac{r^{N,\mathrm{low}}_j}{r^{\mathrm{tw},\mathrm{low}}_j}
   =N_{K/F}\!\left(\frac{u+[j]}{[j]}\right)^{-1}z_j,
\]
and
\[
 \frac{[j]}{r^{\mathrm{tw},\mathrm{high}}_j}
   =N_{K/F}\!\left(\alpha _1\frac{u+[j]}{[j]}\right)^{-1}z_j.
\]
Only after applying the indexed norm character $\mu_j$ and using its
triviality on the displayed norm does Lean obtain, in the corresponding low
and high cases,
\[
 \mu_j(z_j)=\mu_j\!\left(
   \frac{r^N_j}{r^{\mathrm{tw}}_j}\right).
\]
The $j=0$ correction is tied to the selected rows by separate identities;
no indexed-character bridge is asserted at zero.

All of these results are exposed by one cohesive lower-table API.  This node
does not construct a coordinate, prove an upstairs coefficient row, invoke
higher symmetric vanishing, prove a boundary pencil, cancel a residual
phase, or choose a canonical stationary representative.

\end{lemma}
