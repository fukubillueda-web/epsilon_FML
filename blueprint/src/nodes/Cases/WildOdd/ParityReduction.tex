\begin{theorem}[Odd-prime finite phase cancellation]\label{mod:cases-wildodd-parityreduction}
Let $p\ne2$ be prime, let $k$ be the residue field of characteristic $p$,
and let $\psi_0$ be the certified nontrivial residual additive character.
Suppose the completed residual-coefficient table supplies the named generator
and base pairs
\[
 (\eta_0,\eta_0\gamma_0),\qquad (\eta,\eta\gamma),
\]
and, only in the odd boundary case $b=v$ with $v+1$ odd, the boundary
noncancellation conditions $j+d_0\ne0$ for every $j\in\F_p^\times$.
No prime-field shift nonvanishing certificate is required in either strict
branch or in the even boundary case.  Supply also an injective realization of
$\F_p^\times$ in $k^\times$ for the scalar products.
Give a conductor of even parity the residual phase $1$, and give an odd
conductor with coefficient pair $(A,B)$ the complete phase
$Q_{\psi_0}(A,B)$.

Then the following five manuscript patterns hold with their displayed
orientations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item below the break, the odd base phase equals the upper phase;
\item below the break, the product of the odd nonidentity scalar phases is
  $1$;
\item the product of those two below-break contributions has quotient $1$;
\item above the break, in the all-factors-odd pattern the scalar product is
  $1$ and the product of the $p$ repeated twist phases is the upper phase;
\item at the boundary, the complete affine-pencil product is exactly the
  upper phase times the nonidentity generator product.
\end{enumerate}
Consequently, after exhaustive parity dispatch and the disjoint trichotomy
$b<v$, $v<b$, or $b=v$, the complete residual quotient in manuscript
orientation
\[
 U\,\frac{\prod_{j\ne0}R_j}{\prod_jT_j}
\]
is exactly $1$.  The exported assembly API also retains the completed
coefficient table and the certified $q_0^2$, $q_0^{p-1}$, and $q_0^p$
identities used by the scalar and repeated-phase theorems.

\end{theorem}
