\begin{definition}[Wild odd correction units]\label{mod:cases-wildodd-correctionunits}
Let $K/F$ be a cyclic extension of odd prime degree
$p=\operatorname{char}k_F$, with residue degree one.  Fix $T\geq2$ and a
conductor $m$ in the nonstable interval
\[
  2\leq m\leq 2T-1,
  \qquad a=T-m.
\]
The Lean input packages an element $u\in K^\times$ with
$v_K(u)=a$, its norm $n=N_{K/F}(u)$, the exact wild trace-ideal lower
bound with different contribution $(p-1)T$, and the minimal-orbit boundary
condition
\[
  a=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  v_F(n+[j])=0\quad(j\in\mathbf F_p).
\]
Here $[j]$ is always the canonical Teichmüller lift.  Lean separately
defines the integer representative $j_{\mathbf Z}\in\{0,\ldots,p-1\}$;
it proves that $[j]-j_{\mathbf Z}\in\mathfrak p_F$, not that these two
representatives are literally equal in mixed characteristic.  Nonzero
indices are represented by $(\mathbf Z/p\mathbf Z)^\times$, and the exact
equivalence with the subtype $j\ne0$ gives the required finite-product
reindexing.

Put
\[
  u^\vee=-\frac{n}{u}.
\]
The exceptional correction is kept separate from every nonzero index:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lean-wildodd-correction-units}
  z_0=\frac{N_{K/F}(1+u^\vee)}{1+N_{K/F}(u^\vee)},
  \qquad
  z_j=\frac{N_{K/F}(u+[j])}{n+[j]}
  \quad(j\ne0),
\end{equation}
and $x_0=z_0-1$, $x_j=z_j-1$.  In particular, $z_0$ is not obtained by
substituting $j=0$ into the second quotient.  Before any cancellation,
Lean proves
\[
 u\ne0,\quad n\ne0,\quad u^\vee\ne0,\quad
 n+[j]\ne0,\quad 1+N(u^\vee)\ne0,
\]
as well as nonvanishing of both numerators, the full prime-field product,
and $N(n-u)$.  It then packages every $z_0,z_j$ as an element of
$F^\times$ and subsequently proves the stronger principal-unit filtration
statements below.

All parameter identities are literal equalities.  With every sign and
ratio in the manuscript direction, Lean proves
\[
 N(u^\vee)=-n^{p-1},
 \qquad
 N(1+u^\vee)=-\frac{N(n-u)}{n},
 \qquad
 \prod_{j\in\mathbf F_p}(n+[j])=n^p-n,
\]
\[
 N(u+[j])=(n+[j])z_j\quad(j\ne0),
 \qquad
 N(n-u)=\left(\prod_{j\in\mathbf F_p}(n+[j])\right)z_0.
\]
Consequently the interfaces used by the correction formula are exactly
\[
 \frac{\prod_j(n+[j])}{N(n-u)}=z_0^{-1},
 \qquad
 \frac{n+[j]}{[j]}=
 N\!\left(\frac{u+[j]}{[j]}\right)z_j^{-1}
 \quad(j\ne0).
\]
No downstream correction formula or correction-valuation theorem is used.

For
\[
 R(c)=\left\lfloor\frac{c+(p-1)T}{p}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad c\geq0,
\]
Lean defines
\[
 Z(w)=\frac{N(1+w)}{1+N(w)}
\]
and proves the exact identity $Z(w^{-1})=Z(w)$.  After proving the relevant
denominator has valuation zero, it expands
\[
 Z(w)-1=
 \frac{s_1(w)+\cdots+s_{p-1}(w)}{1+N(w)}
\]
and applies the wild symmetric-function bound term by term.  This gives the
manuscript's lower bounds
\[
 v_F(x_j)\geq R(|a|)\quad(j\ne0),
 \qquad
 v_F(x_0)\geq R((p-1)|a|).
\]
They are lower bounds, not asserted valuation equalities.  Finally Lean
proves the exact filtration interfaces
\[
 z_j\in U_F^{R(|a|)}\subseteq U_F^{\lceil T/2\rceil},
 \qquad
 z_0\in U_F^{R((p-1)|a|)}\subseteq U_F^{\lceil m/2\rceil}.
\]

\FormalizationContract The exceptional index $0$ and the nonzero prime-field
indices are separate types and separate definitions.  Every denominator is
proved nonzero before it is canceled.  Prime-field elements in field-valued
formulas are Teichmüller lifts, while natural representatives are used only
through an explicit reduction-compatibility theorem.  Norm and ratio
statements are exact identities with the manuscript signs and directions;
only the depth conclusions are inequalities.  The proof imports neither the
downstream correction formula nor its valuation theorem.
\end{definition}
