\begin{theorem}[Wild odd stable range]\label{mod:cases-wildstable}
Let $K/F$ be a wildly ramified cyclic extension of odd prime degree
$p=[K:F]$, with positive lower break $t$, residue degree one, and a chosen
integral uniformizer $\varpi_K$ generating $\mathcal O_K$ over
$\mathcal O_F$.  Let $\chi_F,\chi_K$ and $\psi_F,\psi_K$ carry proofs of
their exact multiplicative and additive conductors, with the pullbacks in
the literal directions
\[
 \chi_K=\chi_F\circ N_{K/F},
 \qquad
 \psi_K=\psi_F\circ\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F}.
\]
Suppose
\[
 m_F(\chi_F)=2d+\varepsilon>1,
 \qquad
 m_K(\chi_K)=2d_K+\varepsilon_K>1,
 \qquad
 t+1\leq d,
\]
where both decompositions contain the exact parity proofs required by
Lamprecht's formula.  Choose an admissible denominator $\Gamma_F$ and a
representative $\beta$ of the genuine downstairs stationary coefficient
quotient.  The stable-high parameter row maps this supplied representative
to the upstairs quotient, with
\[
 \Gamma_K=\operatorname{alg}_{F\to K}(\Gamma_F),
 \qquad
 \beta_K=\operatorname{alg}_{F\to K}(\beta),
\]
using the ordered, denominator-sensitive pair carried by that row.  No
additional representative is selected internally from either quotient.

Lean's residual input is
\texttt{WildStableResidualComparison} for the actual critical coordinates.
In the even/even branch it is propositionally trivial, because both critical
factors are definitionally $1$.  In the odd/odd branch it is a nonempty
\texttt{RamifiedHasseComparisonData} certificate for exactly the downstairs
phase built from $(\Gamma_F,\beta)$ and the upstairs phase built from
$(\Gamma_K,\beta_K)$.  The public ramified Hasse comparison then proves
\[
 \Delta_{3,K}=\Delta_{3,F}^{\,p}
\]
with positive power orientation.  Thus its wild finite normal form retains
the internal sign $\bar\epsilon=-\bar\lambda^{p-1}$, every residual Hasse
factor, and every representative translation.  Mixed critical parities are
excluded; the conductor relation in the stable-high row and oddness of $p$
give the same parity for the actual conductors.

For every $\mu$ in the complete norm-character group $S(K/F)$, including
its identity, Lean uses proof-bearing exact conductor data
\[
 m_F(1)=0,
 \qquad
 m_F(\mu)=t+1\quad(\mu\ne1).
\]
Hence every conductor is at most $d$.  Stable twisting is applied with the
norm character on the left, to $\mu\chi_F$, and with the unchanged
denominator.  For the supplied stationary representative its orientation is
exactly
\[
 \Delta_F(\mu\chi_F,\psi_F)
 =\mu(\Gamma_F/\beta)\Delta_F(\chi_F,\psi_F).
\]
The inverse involution on the full odd norm-character group proves
$\prod_\mu\mu(\Gamma_F/\beta)=1$, while the norm-character local-constant
product, still including the trivial character, is
$\prod_\mu\Delta_F(\mu,\psi_F)=1$.  Stationary-class transport, the norm and
trace pullbacks, and the residual comparison give
\[
 \Delta_K(\chi_K,\psi_K)
   =\Delta_F(\chi_F,\psi_F)^p.
\]
Combining these identities proves the literal finite-sum First Main Lemma
\[
 \Delta_K^{\mathrm{fin}}(\chi_K,\psi_K;\Gamma_K)
 \prod_{\mu\in S(K/F)}
   \Delta_F^{\mathrm{fin}}(\mu,\psi_F;\Gamma_\mu)
 =
 \prod_{\mu\in S(K/F)}
   \Delta_F^{\mathrm{fin}}(\mu\chi_F,\psi_F;\Gamma_F).
\]

\FormalizationContract
The principal Lean theorem is the displayed \texttt{deltaFinite} product
identity for supplied exact conductor data, admissible denominators, a
downstairs quotient-class representative, parity-critical coordinates, and
the exact residual certificate described above.  It is not an identity for
an arbitrary parameter of type \texttt{LocalConstantFunction}; such a result
requires a separate bridge packaging arbitrary characters with exact
conductor data.  All products range over the complete finite norm-character
group.  The identity character is present on both sides, the stable twist is
left-oriented, and no nonstable case theorem is imported or assumed.
\end{theorem}
