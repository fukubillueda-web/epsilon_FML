\begin{theorem}[Finite products and prime-field sums]\label{mod:basic-finiteproducts}
Let $p$ be prime.  If $R$ is an integral domain and
$\omega:\mathbf F_p^\times\to R^\times$ is an injective homomorphism, then for
every $r\geq 0$,
\[
 \sum_{j\in\mathbf F_p^\times}\omega(j)^r=
 \begin{cases}
  p-1,&p-1\mid r,\\
  0,&p-1\nmid r.
 \end{cases}
\]
If $p\neq2$, then also
$\prod_{j\in\mathbf F_p^\times}\omega(j)=-1$.

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic $p$, and write $j$ for the canonical
image of $j\in\mathbf F_p$ in $K$.  For every $x,\rho\in K$,
\[
 \prod_{j\in\mathbf F_p}(x+j)=x^p-x,
 \qquad
 \prod_{j\in\mathbf F_p}(1+j\rho)=1-\rho^{p-1}.
\]
If $x^p-x\neq0$, then
\[
 \sum_{j\in\mathbf F_p}\frac1{x+j}=\frac1{x-x^p}.
\]
Finally, if $\rho\neq0$ and $\rho^{p-1}\neq1$, then
\[
 \sum_{j\in\mathbf F_p}\frac1{1+j\rho}
   =\frac{\rho^{p-1}}{\rho^{p-1}-1},
 \qquad
 \sum_{j\in\mathbf F_p}\frac{j}{1+j\rho}
   =-\frac{\rho^{p-2}}{\rho^{p-1}-1};
\]
when $p\neq2$ one further has
\[
 \sum_{j\in\mathbf F_p}\frac{j^2}{1+j\rho}
   =\frac{\rho^{p-3}}{\rho^{p-1}-1}.
\]
Finite reindexing itself is supplied by Mathlib's sum and product lemmas for
finite equivalences; the declarations here are the prime-field and
logarithmic-derivative specializations used downstream.
