\begin{theorem}[Low-conductor stationary-class table]
\label{mod:parameters-low}
Let $K/F$ be a ramified cyclic extension of prime degree $p$ with lower
break $t$, residue degree one, and the supplied integral uniformizer and
monogenicity data used to enumerate all norm characters.  Put
\[
 T=t+1,
 \qquad r_0=\left\lfloor\frac T2\right\rfloor,
 \qquad s_0=\left\lceil\frac T2\right\rceil .
\]
Let $\chi_F$ be minimal in its complete norm-character orbit and assume
the exact low-conductor decomposition
\[
 2\leq m=m_F(\chi_F)=2d+e\leq T,
 \qquad e\leq1,
 \qquad q=d+e.
\]
For exact pullback data
\[
 \chi_K=\chi_F\circ N_{K/F},
 \qquad
 \psi_K=\psi_F\circ\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F},
\]
Lean first proves the actual conductor identity
\[
 m_K(\chi_K)=m.
\]
For every norm character $\mu$, not merely for a chosen enumeration, it
also proves
\[
 m_F(\mu\chi_F)=
 \begin{cases}
 m,&\mu=1,\\
 T,&\mu\ne1.
 \end{cases}
\]
Thus the identity row is never assigned conductor $T$ when $m<T$, while
minimality excludes all cancellation in the nonidentity rows, including
the boundary $m=T$.

Choose denominators
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_F(\delta)=T+n_F(\psi_F),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{ord}_K(\epsilon_1)=v:=T-m,
\]
and define the exact norm and the two ordered stationary denominators by
\[
 \eta=N_{K/F}(\epsilon_1),
 \qquad
 \gamma_F=\delta/\eta,
 \qquad
 \gamma_K=\delta/\epsilon_1.
\]
Their required valuations are derived in Lean.  Fix the generator $\tau$
of the complete cyclic norm-character enumeration.  Its stationary class
relative to $\delta$ belongs to
$\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak p_F^{r_0}$, whereas the native class of $\chi_F$
relative to $\gamma_F$ belongs to
$\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak p_F^d$.  Lean proves exactly
\[
 r_0\leq t,
 \qquad d\leq t,
\]
and constructs, in one simultaneous existential package, independent
subcritical representatives
\[
 \alpha_1,\beta_1\in K^\times,
 \qquad
 \alpha=N_{K/F}(\alpha_1),
 \qquad
 \beta=N_{K/F}(\beta_1),
\]
whose norm congruences have precisely the two allowed depths $r_0$ and
$d$.  The package records the two supplied quotient classes and does not
choose a canonical field representative.  For two such packages, the
choice-independence theorem asserts only
\[
 \alpha\equiv\alpha'\pmod{\mathfrak p_F^{r_0}},
 \qquad
 \beta\equiv\beta'\pmod{\mathfrak p_F^d},
\]
together with the corresponding target congruences and source-ratio
memberships
\[
 \alpha_1/\alpha_1'\in U_K^{r_0},
 \qquad
 \beta_1/\beta_1'\in U_K^d.
\]
No equality of the field representatives is asserted.

For every simultaneous package, the upstairs stationary numerator class
relative to $\gamma_K$ is the quotient equality
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \beta_K=
 \left[
   \frac{\beta\eta}{\epsilon_1}
   -\frac{\beta_1\alpha}{\alpha_1}
 \right]
 \quad}
 \qquad\text{in }\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_K^d.
\]
This is proved from the exact truncated norm and
\texttt{stationaryClass\_compNorm}; it is not promoted to an equality in
$K$.  Norms of two supplied representatives of this same upstairs class
are congruent modulo $\mathfrak p_F^d$, using the ambiguity inclusion of
the same low norm-polynomial precision certificate.

For $j\in\mathbf F_p$, write $[j]$ for the prime Teichmuller lift and let
$\widetilde\beta_j$ be the common-denominator class.  The table is
heterogeneous at $j=0$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $j=0$ retains actual conductor $m$ and its native class in
  $\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak p_F^d$ is transported by multiplication by
  $\eta$ to the common layer;
\item $j\ne0$ has actual conductor $T$ and hence an ordinary stationary
  class relative to $\delta$ in
  $\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak p_F^{r_0}$.
\end{itemize}
In that common quotient Lean proves every row of
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \widetilde\beta_j
 \equiv N_{K/F}([j]\alpha_1+\epsilon_1\beta_1)
 \equiv [j]\alpha+\eta\beta
 \pmod{\mathfrak p_F^{r_0}}.
 \quad}
\]
The mixed-characteristic proof uses the Teichmuller lift itself.  Its
comparison with the cyclic natural-number power occurs only in the
stationary quotient at the justified half-break precision; no equality
between $[j]$ and the integer $j$ in $F$ is assumed.

Put
\[
 u=\frac{\epsilon_1\beta_1}{\alpha_1},
 \qquad
 n=N_{K/F}(u)=\frac{\eta\beta}{\alpha},
 \qquad
 \beta_K^0=\frac{\alpha}{\epsilon_1}(n-u).
\]
Lean retains the exact identities for $\operatorname{ord}_K(u)$ and
$N_{K/F}(u)$ and proves, with no stronger target, the product congruence
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 N_{K/F}(\beta_K^0)
 \equiv
 \beta\prod_{j\in\mathbf F_p^\times}
   (\eta\beta+[j]\alpha)
 \pmod{\mathfrak p_F^d}.
 \quad}
\]
The proof controls every nonterminal elementary-symmetric term and then
uses the exact Teichmuller polynomial identity.  The denominator is
exactly the common denominator $\delta$, and the target ambiguity is
exactly $d$; neither is weakened.

Finally, Lean exposes the pre-minimal critical cancellation trichotomy
needed to prevent misuse of an old-layer sum.  For an arbitrary critical
product, either its actual conductor remains $T$ and its leading quotient
class is nonzero, or its conductor drops to some $m'$ with
$1<m'<T$ and a new stationary class is constructed at the actual
conductor and minimal half-depth before restriction to the old layer, or
$m'\leq1$ and no stationary class exists.  Conductors zero and one are
also exported as a separate endpoint theorem.

\FormalizationContract
The principal declaration returns the simultaneous representative package
existentially together with quotient equalities for the native classes,
the common twist layer, and the upstairs class.  The identity twist keeps
conductor $m$; only nonidentity twists have conductor $T$.  Conductor drops
are handled only by reconstructing the class at the new actual depth, and
the zero/one endpoints create no class.  All occurrences of
$\alpha_1,\beta_1,\alpha,\beta,$ and $\beta_K^0$ are supplied witnesses;
none is canonical.  Choice independence and every displayed stationary
identity are stated only as lattice-quotient equalities, filtration
membership, or congruences at the manuscript's exact depths.  The file
imports none of the high-parameter, phase-reduction, or later case
theorems.
\end{theorem}
