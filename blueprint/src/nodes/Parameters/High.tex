\begin{theorem}[Public high-conductor stationary-class table]
\label{mod:parameters-high}
The public high-conductor interface is a universal record whose five named
fields are precisely the five mathematical rows below.  Each field quantifies
its own local fields and hypotheses, so the unramified row is not tied to any
ramified lower-break or monogenicity data.  Put
\[
  T=t+1,
  \qquad m=a(\chi_F)=2d+\varepsilon,
  \qquad m_K=a(\chi_K)=2d_K+\varepsilon_K.
\]
Lean first records the prime-degree alternatives
\[
 e(K/F)=1\quad\hbox{or}\quad f(K/F)=1,
 \qquad
 [K:F]=2\quad\hbox{or}\quad [K:F]\text{ is odd}.
\]
For every stationary decomposition in the odd ramified high range
$T\leq m$, Lean proves the disjoint numerical split
\[
 d\geq T
 \quad\hbox{or}\quad
 t>0\ \hbox{ and }\ T\leq m<2T.
\]
Thus the stable and odd non-stable rows have neither a gap nor an overlap.
For the quadratic rows Lean records the exhaustive split $t=0$ or $t>0$.

The five fields have the following exact contents.
\begin{enumerate}
\item In the unramified row, $e(K/F)=1$ is an explicit hypothesis.  Lean
proves
\[
 a(\chi_K)=a(\chi_F),
 \qquad n_K(\psi_K)=n_F(\psi_F),
\]
retains the actual upstairs decomposition $(d,\varepsilon)$, and proves that
the image of an admissible $\gamma_F$ is admissible for $\chi_K,\psi_K$.
For the ordered pair
$(\gamma_F,\operatorname{algebraMap}_{F,K}\gamma_F)$, it packages the
literal quotient equality
\[
 \operatorname{St}_K(\chi_K,\psi_K;\gamma_K)
 =\operatorname{denominatorScaledAlgebraMap}_{d,d}
   \bigl(\operatorname{St}_F(\chi_F,\psi_F;\gamma_F)\bigr)
 \quad\text{in }\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_K^d.
\]
No equality of field representatives is asserted.

\item In the totally ramified odd-degree stable row, residue degree one is
an explicit hypothesis and the boundary is exactly $d\geq T$.  Lean exposes
both forms of the actual upstairs conductor:
\[
 m_K=\psi_{t,[K:F]}(m-1)+1,
 \qquad
 m_K+([K:F]-1)T=[K:F]m.
\]
It proves admissibility of the same ordered common-denominator pair and
returns the exact depth certificate $d_K\leq e(K/F)d$ together with
\[
 \operatorname{St}_K(\chi_K,\psi_K;\gamma_K)
 =\operatorname{denominatorScaledAlgebraMap}_{d,d_K}
   \bigl(\operatorname{St}_F(\chi_F,\psi_F;\gamma_F)\bigr)
 \quad\text{in }\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_K^{d_K}.
\]
The target and source stationary decompositions are the supplied actual
decompositions; no alternate depth is substituted.

\item In the tamely ramified quadratic row the exact assumptions are
$[K:F]=2$, $t=0$, residue degree one, and residue characteristic different
from two.  Lean proves
\[
 m_K+1=2m,
 \qquad (d_K,\varepsilon_K)=(m-1,1),
 \qquad d_K+\varepsilon_K=m.
\]
It retains the same common denominator and returns
$m-1\leq e(K/F)d$ and the corresponding quotient-class image in
$\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_K^{m-1}$.  In particular, the upstairs stationary
source is exactly the layer at depth $m$, not a convenient deeper layer.

\item In the positive-break odd non-stable row, the range is exactly
\[
 0<t,
 \qquad T\leq m<2T,
\]
and $\chi_F$ is minimal in its complete norm-character orbit.  Lean retains
the actual identities
\[
 m_K=T+[K:F](m-T),
 \qquad
 a(\mu\chi_F)=m\quad\text{for every norm character }\mu,
\]
including the identity character and the endpoint $m=T$.  It returns the
exact norm-polynomial precision for
$(m_K,d_K,\varepsilon_K;m,d,\varepsilon)$ and independent existential
witnesses
\[
 a,b,\alpha,\beta,\alpha_1,\beta_1.
\]
The two norm-representative certificates have precisely the allowed
precisions
\[
 \left(m-T,\left\lfloor\frac T2\right\rfloor\right)
 \quad\text{and}\quad (0,d);
\]
they are congruence-and-ambiguity certificates, not definitions of canonical
field lifts.  The package keeps the numerator class of the index-one norm
character in
$\mathfrak p_F^{m-T}/\mathfrak p_F^{m-T+\lfloor T/2\rfloor}$ and the
coefficient class of $\chi_F$ in
$\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak p_F^d$.

For $y\in\mathfrak p_K^{\lceil T/2\rceil}$, the stored conversion has the
exact direction and sign
\[
 \psi_F\!\left(\frac{N(\alpha_1)N(y)}{\gamma_F}\right)
 =
 \psi_F\!\left(\frac{-N(\alpha_1)\operatorname{Tr}(y)}{\gamma_F}\right),
\]
where both norm and trace point from $K$ to $F$.  With the displayed ordered
denominator pair, the supplied integral candidate
\[
 \operatorname{algebraMap}_{F,K}N(\beta_1)
 -\beta_1\frac{\operatorname{algebraMap}_{F,K}N(\alpha_1)}{\alpha_1}
\]
represents both the denominator-sensitive adjoint of the downstairs class
and the actual upstairs stationary class in
$\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_K^{d_K}$.  For every
$j\in\mathbf F_{[K:F]}$, including $j=0$, the package retains the actual
twist datum and denominator and proves, only in
$\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak p_F^d$, that
\[
 [N(\beta_1+[j]\alpha_1)]
 =[N(\beta_1)+[j]N(\alpha_1)]
 =\operatorname{St}(\tau^j\chi_F,\psi_F;\gamma_F).
\]

\item In the positive-break wild quadratic row the exact range is
$t>0$ and $m\geq T$, with degree two, residue degree one, and whole-orbit
minimality.  Lean returns a nonempty
\texttt{WildQuadraticHighParameterData} package.  In particular it retains
\[
 m_K+T=2m,
 \qquad d_K=m-T+\left\lfloor\frac T2\right\rfloor,
 \qquad \varepsilon_K=T\bmod 2,
\]
the exact common denominator and norm-polynomial precision, the exact
conductor $T$ of the nontrivial norm character, and the actual conductor $m$
of its twist with $\chi_F$.

Its representatives are simultaneous existential choices with
\[
 \beta=\delta_hN(\beta_1),
 \qquad \delta_h\in U_F^t,
 \qquad \alpha=N(\alpha_1)
\]
where $\beta_1$ comes only from the break-level norm theorem and $\alpha_1$
only from precision $\lfloor T/2\rfloor$.  The factor $\delta_h$ is retained
in the numerator $c_F=\alpha\delta_h$, the selected upstairs representative
\[
 \beta_K^0=\beta-\beta_1c_F/\alpha_1,
\]
the twist representative $\beta+c_F$, and all associated quotient-class,
adjoint, trace, norm, nonvanishing, and boundary-noncancellation statements.
No representative or characteristic-two refinement is made canonical.
\end{enumerate}

The table separately exposes the exact critical cancellation API used by the
odd row.  At $m=T$, whole-orbit minimality gives a nonzero leading quotient
class for every nonidentity norm character.  Before minimality, if an actual
product conductor $q'$ satisfies
\[
 1<q'<T,
\]
Lean constructs its stationary class at the new conductor $q'$ and its new
stationary depth before restricting it to the old critical layer.  If the
actual conductor is zero or one, Lean proves that no stationary depth exists
and constructs no stationary class.

Finally, the field \texttt{highProduct} is exactly the two-branch
\texttt{HighProductBranch} theorem of the completed product node.  Norm is
induced only on the certified unit quotients
\[
 U_K^0/U_K^{d_K}\longrightarrow U_F^0/U_F^d,
\]
with source denominator $d_K$ and target denominator $d$; it is not applied
to an arbitrary additive quotient class.  In the odd branch the quotient
product is indexed by all $j\in\mathbf F_{[K:F]}$ and its field reading is
only the depth-$d$ congruence
\[
 N(\beta_K^0)\equiv
 N(\beta_1)^{[K:F]}-
 N(\beta_1)N(\alpha_1)^{[K:F]-1}
 \pmod{\mathfrak p_F^d}.
\]
In the wild quadratic branch the two factors are the original and
nontrivial-twist classes and the exact stored congruence is
\[
 N(\beta_K^0)\equiv
 \beta\bigl(\beta+N(\alpha_1)\delta_h\bigr)
 \pmod{\mathfrak p_F^d}.
\]
The correction unit $\delta_h$ is never discarded.  Representative
independence is asserted only at depths $d_K$ upstairs and $d$ downstairs.

\FormalizationContract
The principal declaration is a universal proposition-valued record.  Its
five case fields preserve the component theorems' exact hypotheses,
conductor boundaries, actual stationary decompositions, ordered denominator
pairs, quotient types, and norm/trace/adjoint directions.  The odd
intermediate field contains the literal dependent conclusion of the
completed component theorem, rather than a simplified field-valued formula.
The wild quadratic field contains the completed simultaneous existential
package with its indispensable unit correction.  The critical and product
fields are auxiliary interfaces, not additional high-conductor cases.
No quotient equality is promoted to an equality in $F$ or $K$, no canonical
stationary representative is introduced, no cancelled old-layer sum is
reused at a dropped conductor, and no low-parameter, phase-reduction,
Ramified-Hasse-comparison, or later case theorem is imported or used.
\end{theorem}
