\begin{definition}[Exact reduction to residual phases]
\label{mod:parameters-phasereduction}
Let $m=a(\chi_F)$ and $T=t+1$.  The phase-reduction interface uses the
disjoint assembly convention
\[
  m\leq T\quad\text{(low)},
  \qquad
  T<m\quad\text{(high)}.
\]
In particular $m=T$ belongs to the low branch.  Lean proves that every
pair $(t,m)$ belongs to exactly one of these branches and forwards the
complete high- and low-parameter tables without weakening their dependent
hypotheses.

For a stationary decomposition
\[
  a(\chi)=2d+\varepsilon,
  \qquad \varepsilon\leq1,
\]
an input representative is a pair consisting of
\[
  c\in\mathcal O_E,
  \qquad
  [c]=\operatorname{StCoeff}_E(\chi,\psi;\gamma)
       \quad\text{in }\mathcal O_E/\mathfrak p_E^d.
\]
This is a quotient-class package, not a choice of a canonical element of
$E$.  Two supplied packages for the same class are proved congruent exactly
modulo $\mathfrak p_E^d$.  The exact norm representatives $\alpha=N(\alpha_1)$
and $\beta=N(\beta_1)$ from the low table are converted to such packages
only through the representatives already supplied by that table.

The parity-indexed critical-coordinate type has no field datum in even
conductor.  In odd conductor it contains precisely a supplied
$\delta\in E^\times$ satisfying $v_E(\delta)=d$.  Combining such a coordinate
with a supplied stationary quotient representative inserts the literal
stationary numerator class into the corresponding even or odd branch of
Lamprecht's formula.  No stationary package exists at conductors zero or
one.

Lean fixes one residual-coordinate source from the uniformizer $\varpi_K$
already supplied to the parameter tables and puts
$\varpi_F=N_{K/F}(\varpi_K)$.  Residue degree one proves that both have
valuation one.  At depth $r$ the source coordinates are
\[
 c_K(r)=\varpi_K^r,
 \qquad c_F(r)=\varpi_F^r=N_{K/F}\bigl(c_K(r)\bigr).
\]
The parity dispatcher contains no field coordinate in the even case and
uses exactly the corresponding source coordinate at the actual depth in the
odd case.  Residue degree one also upgrades the canonical extension residue
map to a ring equivalence $k_F\simeq k_K$; no independent residue-field
identification is selected.

Lean also defines a quotient-correct residual-class normalizer.  An element
$y\in\mathfrak p_E^r$ is divided by the $r$th power of the fixed source
uniformizer only as an element of the integral lattice and is then reduced
modulo $\mathfrak p_E$; no representative of a stationary quotient is
chosen.  Equality modulo $\mathfrak p_E^{r+1}$ gives equality of these
classes.  Applied to the actual norm polynomial
$P(x)=N_{K/F}(1+x)-1$, the Low table gives the base class $z^p$ in the
natural upper coordinate, the boundary norm-row class
$\overline{N(u)}z^p$, and a zero norm-row class in the strict-low range.
In the strict-high range both the base and norm-row values lie one step
beyond their genuine post-drop critical depths, so both residual classes
are zero.  The depth statements are proved for arbitrary upper elements of
the required order and are then specialized to the literal source-coordinate
Teichm\"uller lift; no second upper representative is identified with it.

For every actual local Lamprecht package $L$, Lean exposes the complete
residual function itself: it is the constant-one parity convention in even
conductor and the quotient-representative-derived Lamprecht Hasse function
in odd conductor.  Relative to a supplied nontrivial residual additive
character, its polar coefficient $A$ and, in odd residue characteristic,
its affine coefficient $B$ are extracted from that same function.  Lean
proves
\[
 \Phi_L(x)=\psi_0(Ax^2/2+Bx)
\]
and identifies the previously used critical factor with the phase of the
finite sum of $\Phi_L$.  A source-tied-row constructor applies an actual
High or Low row constructor to the coordinate forced by the common source
and exports both this complete function and its finite-sum phase.

If a selected odd critical coordinate $c$ has the same exact order as the
source coordinate $c_0$, Lean derives the integral unit $u_c=c/c_0$ and its
residue $\lambda$.  In the source-to-selected direction it proves
\[
 \Phi_c(x)=\Phi_{c_0}(\lambda x),
 \qquad A_c=\lambda^2A_{c_0},
 \qquad B_c=\lambda B_{c_0}.
\]
For two supplied representatives $\beta,\beta'$ of the same stationary
quotient class at the actual depth, it proves, with the displayed positive
orientations,
\[
 \Phi_{\beta'}(x)=\Phi_\beta(x)\psi_0(\Delta Bx),
 \qquad B_{\beta'}=B_\beta+A a.
\]
This function-level statement accompanies the existing transport of the
elementary factor and Hasse phase together.  Lean also proves reusable
complete-function identities for a literal sum representative of a product
character, for a secondary character already linearized on the common
critical line, and for a literal scalar multiple representing a character
power.  These are transport identities, not the residual coefficient table.

At the field-character level Lean keeps the exact polynomial
\[
 P(y)=N_{K/F}(1+y)-1,
 \qquad H(y)=P(y)-\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F}(y).
\]
From $N(u)=n$ it proves, before any residual coefficient calculation, the
complete identity
\[
 \Phi_K(x)=\Phi_\chi(P(x))\,\Phi_\tau(P(ux))^{-1}
   \psi_F\!\left(\mathcal A\bigl(H(ux)-nH(x)\bigr)\right),
\]
with each displayed function evaluated through its actual stationary
numerator-to-denominator ratio and with the norm character killed only on
the literal norm of $1+ux$.  The High and Low specializations derive the
three ratios $\mathcal A$, $\mathcal A n$, and
$\mathcal A(n-u)$ from their real table representatives, including the Low
$\varepsilon_1$ correction.  This is an equality of complete functions;
it neither asserts nor uses the downstream coefficient table.

For a residue-field equivalence $e:k\simeq l$, Lean defines the reindexed
complete function by
\[
 (e_*\Phi)(y)=\Phi(e^{-1}y).
\]
It proves that the coefficient pair becomes $(eA,eB)$ and that the complete
finite sum and its phase are unchanged.  The canonical upper-to-lower
transport therefore evaluates the upper function at $e(x)$ and applies
$e^{-1}$ to both coefficients.  The displayed upper coordinate then
precomposes this lower-field function by inverse Frobenius.  If
$\operatorname{Fr}(x)=x^p$, Lean proves
\[
 \Phi^{\mathrm{disp}}(z)=
   \Phi\bigl(\operatorname{Fr}^{-1}z\bigr),
 \qquad (A,B)\longmapsto(A^p,B^p),
\]
where the affine statement has the stated odd-characteristic hypothesis;
the complete sum and phase are again unchanged.  The combined source-tied
displayed upper function consequently has coefficient pair
$((e^{-1}A)^p,(e^{-1}B)^p)$.  These reindexing declarations fix the
Frobenius direction and introduce no additional rowwise scaling scalar.

For the actual upper row, the residual additive-character datum stores one
nontrivial lower character $\psi_0$ together with its proved Frobenius
invariance.  Its upper character is derived, not supplied independently, by
pulling $\psi_0$ through the inverse of the canonical residue equivalence.
Lean proves both the compatibility
\[
 \psi_{0,K}(e(x))=\psi_0(x)
\]
and nontriviality of this derived upper character.  Consequently an actual
upper Lamprecht package $L_K$ has a displayed complete function in the lower
residue coordinate with the exact value
\[
 \Phi^{\mathrm{disp}}_{L_K}(x)=
 \Phi_{L_K}\!\left(e\bigl(\operatorname{Fr}^{-1}x\bigr)\right).
\]
Lean proves equality of its finite sum and phase with those of the original
actual upper function.  Its polar coefficient is
$\bigl(e^{-1}A_K\bigr)^p$, and under the odd-characteristic hypotheses its
affine coefficient is $\bigl(e^{-1}B_K\bigr)^p$.  Thus the additive
character, residue equivalence, complete function, and coefficient
directions in the common displayed coordinate are all derived from the
actual row data.  The named High and Low coefficient wrappers accept this
one lower character datum: their upper character is its derived pullback and
every lower norm, base, and twist wrapper uses its lower character.  Thus
those wrappers cannot be instantiated with independently rescaled upper and
lower residual-character coordinates.

In the stable high row for odd prime degree, an explicitly supplied
downstairs quotient representative $c$ is sent upstairs as the literal field element
$\iota_{F\to K}(c)$.  The existential depth assertion in the proposition-valued
high table is eliminated only to prove that this element represents the
upstairs quotient class; it is not used to select an element.  Lean packages
the downstairs and upstairs Lamprecht phases with their separate actual
conductors, depths, and critical coordinates, without identifying the two
quotient types or asserting that their critical phases agree.

The other high-product adapters likewise use the exact quotient
representatives of the high table.  In the odd non-stable branch they insert
the upstairs class and every indexed twist class at the decomposition carried
by that twist.  One coherent source is obtained by destructuring the
intermediate-high parameter theorem once: its norm rows and product rows have
literally the same supplied $\alpha$ and $\alpha_1$.  From that source every
nonidentity norm-character row is constructed at conductor $t+1$: its
denominator is exactly $\gamma_F/N_{K/F}(\alpha_1)$, and its representative
is the corresponding nonzero Teichm\"uller scalar in the stationary
coefficient quotient at depth $\lfloor(t+1)/2\rfloor$.  The selected-$\alpha$
linearization and the proved norm order justify this exact-norm replacement;
Lean does not invent an equality $\alpha=N_{K/F}(\alpha_1)$.

For these High norm rows Lean also retains the distinction between the
table's Teichm\"uller representative $[j]$ and the literal natural multiple
$j$.  At the actual dropped conductor $t+1$ and depth
$\lfloor(t+1)/2\rfloor$, it proves separately that $1$, $j$, and $[j]$
represent the generator, literal powered, and actual indexed stationary
classes for the denominator $\gamma_F/N(\alpha_1)$.  The named
\texttt{highSourceNormFunction} is proved to be the complete function of
the actual $[j]$ row.  Lean then constructs the exact representative-change
datum from the literal $j$ row to the actual $[j]$ row, proves
\[
 \Phi_{[j]}(X)=\Phi_j(X)\psi_0(\Delta B_jX),
\]
and proves that their polar coefficients agree.  Finally it proves
$\Phi_j(X)=\Phi_1(X)^j$ for the literal rows.  Thus the High power law is
not obtained by identifying $[j]$ with $j$, and the affine translation is
not discarded.

For a twist index $j$, the exact-norm representative supplied by the product
table is congruent at its actual stationary depth to the linear value
\[
  c_j^{\mathrm{lin}}=N(\beta_1)+[j]N(\alpha_1)
    =N(\alpha_1)(n+[j]),
  \qquad n=N(\beta_1/\alpha_1).
\]
Lean therefore builds a second quotient representative from this literal
linear value.  In odd conductor this replacement is inserted into the
complete Lamprecht package, so the elementary value and its translated Hasse
phase travel together.

In the wild quadratic branch the adapters insert the upstairs, base,
nontrivial norm-character, and twist classes from one simultaneous
high-product choice;
the norm-character row uses the literal representative $1$ at conductor
$t+1$ with the changed denominator supplied by the table.  The public high
non-stable adapters require the strict assembly inequality $t+1<m$.  The selected
nontrivial-twist representative retains the exact correction
\[
  \beta+N_{K/F}(\alpha_1)\delta_h,
  \qquad \delta_h\in U_F^t;
\]
$\delta_h$ is not replaced by one.  The public high-table adapters return
these phase packages together from the same existential choice.

For the low table, the base and norm-character-generator packages use the
explicitly supplied $\beta$ and $\alpha$.  For every nonzero cyclic index
$j$, Lean proves that the Teichmüller multiple $[j]\alpha$ represents the
stationary class of $\tau^j$ at conductor $t+1$ and the exact critical
half-depth, and inserts that class into Lamprecht's formula.  The upstairs representative and
each nonzero twist representative occur under existential fields of a
proposition-valued table, so Lean returns only
\texttt{Nonempty} phase packages and never eliminates those witnesses into
a canonical Type-valued choice.  Every nonzero twist uses the newly proved
conductor-$T$ decomposition and the actual depth $\lfloor T/2\rfloor$; the
identity row retains its conductor $m$.

In the quadratic low branch the nonidentity norm row is also exposed in the
manuscript normalization
\[
  (\delta,\alpha)\longmapsto(\delta/\alpha,1).
\]
The representative $1$ is proved to represent the changed-denominator class;
it is not selected from a quotient.  Its inverse multiplicative elementary
factor is literally $1$, its positive additive argument is
$1/(\delta/\alpha)=\alpha/\delta$, and its complete Lamprecht factor equals
the factor for the original pair.  The equality $\tau(\alpha)=1$ is derived
from the table's exact norm witness $\alpha=N(\alpha_1)$.

Lean proves that proof-bearing additive-character data are determined by
their underlying character, and transports a stationary Lamprecht package
only along equalities of the underlying multiplicative and additive
characters.  This transport changes neither the stationary quotient
representative nor its denominator, conductor, depth, critical coordinate,
or admissible factor.  Consequently every high- and low-table upstairs
adapter has the exact computational target, and the table-backed records
identify it with the upstairs row of $D$ by ordinary equality, never by
heterogeneous equality.

For a stationary local package $L$, write
\[
 A(L)=\chi(\gamma),\qquad
 M_+(L)=\text{its positive additive value},\qquad
 M_\times(L)=\text{its inverse multiplicative-character value},
\]
so that $M(L)=M_+(L)M_\times(L)$, and write
\[
 H(L)=\begin{cases}
  1,&a(\chi)\text{ even},\\
  \text{the Lamprecht Hasse-sum phase},&a(\chi)\text{ odd}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Lean proves the exact factorization
\[
  \Delta_{\mathrm{fin}}(\chi,\psi)
   =A(L)M(L)H(L).
\]
At endpoint conductors the local sum is retained literally and no
stationary class is manufactured.  In even conductor the elementary factor
is independent of the supplied representative at depth $d$.  In odd
conductor Lean transports the elementary value and the translated Hasse
phase together and proves independence only for their product.  It also
exports the explicit translation statement; neither member is discarded in
isolation.  In both parities the complete local Lamprecht factor is then
proved independent of the representative, using that paired odd result.
The additive and inverse-character halves of the elementary factor are
exposed separately, but no representative-independence claim separates them
from the translated Hasse factor in odd conductor.

Let $D$ contain the local phase package upstairs and the local packages for
every norm character $\mu$ and every twist $\mu\chi_F$.  Lean separates the
error term into four oriented quotients
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E(D)&=
 \frac{E_K\prod_\mu E_\mu}{\prod_\mu E_{\mu\chi_F}},&
 A(D)&=
 \frac{A_K\prod_\mu A_\mu}{\prod_\mu A_{\mu\chi_F}},\\
 M(D)&=
 \frac{M_K\prod_\mu M_\mu}{\prod_\mu M_{\mu\chi_F}},&
 H(D)&=
 \frac{H_K\prod_\mu H_\mu}{\prod_\mu H_{\mu\chi_F}}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Here $E_*$ denotes the literal endpoint factor and the other symbols have
the meanings above.  Every denominator is proved nonzero.  Expanding only
the already-proved definition of the error term gives
\[
  \operatorname{Err}_{K/F}(\chi_F)=E(D)A(D)M(D)H(D).
\]
The result also forwards the already-proved invariance under multiplication
of $\chi_F$ by a norm character.  It nowhere assumes that this error term is
one.  Lean further splits the elementary quotient, with its orientation
unchanged, as
\[
 M(D)=M_+(D)M_\times(D),\qquad
 M_+(D)=\frac{M_{+,K}\prod_\mu M_{+,\mu}}
                  {\prod_\mu M_{+,\mu\chi_F}},\qquad
 M_\times(D)=\frac{M_{\times,K}\prod_\mu M_{\times,\mu}}
                  {\prod_\mu M_{\times,\mu\chi_F}}.
\]

For odd prime degree, the exact assembly record is tied to the computational
base datum and carries an equivalence
$\{\ast\}\sqcup I\simeq S(K/F)$ sending $\ast$ to the identity.  Thus $I$
enumerates every nonidentity norm character exactly once, and each indexed
norm row is an actual stationary package in $D$.  Its cancellable raw
endpoint and admissible numerators and denominators are separately identified
with common nonzero factors.  Its elementary numerator is a common nonzero
factor and its elementary denominator is
\[
 \left[
  \psi_F\!\left(A\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F}(u)\right)
  \chi_F(z_0)\prod_i\tau_i(z_i)
 \right]
 \times\text{that common factor}.
\]
Consequently, without cancelling the residual critical phase,
\[
 \operatorname{Err}_{K/F}(\chi_F)
 =H(D)
 \left[
  \psi_F\!\left(A\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F}(u)\right)
  \chi_F(z_0)\prod_i\tau_i(z_i)
 \right]^{-1}.
\]
The record retains the exact norm identity $N(u)=n$, the explicit scalar
lift attached to each norm-character index, and
\[
 X=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/F}(u)+n(z_0-1)
      +\sum_i [i](z_i-1).
\]
If the two pointwise linearizations
\[
 \chi_F(z_0)=\psi_F\bigl(An(z_0-1)\bigr),
 \qquad
 \tau_i(z_i)=\psi_F\bigl(A[i](z_i-1)\bigr),
\]
are supplied as separate hypotheses, Lean derives the second exact
orientation
\[
 \operatorname{Err}_{K/F}(\chi_F)=H(D)\psi_F(-AX).
\]
This is the formal content corresponding to the exact odd assembly; no
finite-field cancellation of $H(D)$ is performed.

For a prime cyclic extension Lean also exports the concrete index type
\[
 I=\{j\in\mathbf Z/[K:F]\mathbf Z:j\ne0\}
\]
and constructs the displayed option equivalence from the ramified
norm-character equivalence.  The scalar in the correction sum is fixed to
the literal Teichm\"uller lift $[j]$; it is not an arbitrary field-valued
input.  More precisely, the manuscript correction units are definitions
\[
 z_0=\frac{N(n-u)}{\prod_{k\in\mathbf Z/[K:F]\mathbf Z}(n+[k])},
 \qquad
 z_j=\frac{N(u+[j])}{n+[j]}\quad(j\ne0).
\]
The corresponding Lean record contains only $N(u)=n$ and the nonvanishing
facts needed to bundle these literal ratios as units.  It has no fields for
arbitrary replacement values of $z_0$ or $z_j$.  For $j\ne0$, the selected
norm-row to twist-row ratio is not $z_j$.  In the coherent high source it is
\[
 N_{K/F}\!\left(\alpha_1\frac{u+[j]}{[j]}\right)^{-1}z_j,
\]
and in the low source it is
\[
 N_{K/F}\!\left(\frac{u+[j]}{[j]}\right)^{-1}z_j.
\]
Only after applying the actual indexed norm character does Lean use
triviality on norms and obtain
\[
 \tau^j(z_j)=\tau^j(\text{selected row ratio}).
\]
The zero index is separate: its full product denominator, nonvanishing
statement, and selected-row identity belong to $z_0$, and no nonidentity
character bridge is asserted for $j=0$.

The named high source rows are the actual upstairs row, every nonidentity
norm row at its post-drop conductor and depth, and every twist row, including
$j=0$, at its genuine base conductor.  Their phase packages and complete

the literal
correction-unit package above,
$X$ with its displayed defining equality, and the separate pointwise

When the high base conductor is odd, Lean proves from the actual selected
$\alpha_1$ linearization that every indexed high twist function is pointwise
equal to the actual zero-index/base function in the common lower source
coordinate.  The proof first identifies the literal representatives
$N(\beta_1)+[j]N(\alpha_1)$ and transports the indexed norm character and

The named low source rows are the actual upstairs row, the separate $j=0$
base row at its own conductor, and every nonidentity norm and twist row at

low odd constructor requires the explicit odd degree hypothesis, the
supplied low pair, the existential upstairs and nonzero-twist witnesses,
their named phase-row equalities, and the same minimal correction input.  It
internally derives all raw and aggregate row algebra from those witnesses.
Both constructors select the strict-high or inclusive-low range
definitionally and derive the endpoint quotient $1$, including the trivial
norm-character local constant.  Their preferred result eliminators contain
no independently supplied upper, base, norm-row, or twist-row critical
coordinates.

Lean further proves the complete lower-function identities forced by those
literal rows.  In the strict-low range, where the base character is already
linearized on the deeper line, this holds at either permitted conductor
parity (both functions are the literal constant-one Lamprecht function in
the even case):
\[
  \Phi_{\tau^j\chi_F}(X)=\Phi_{\tau^j}(X)
  \qquad(j\ne0).
\]
At the boundary conductor, where the two actual characters have the same

\[
  \Phi_{\tau^j\chi_F}(X)
    =\Phi_{\tau^j}(X)\Phi_{\chi_F}(X).
\]
Both statements use the common lower source coordinate and the actual
High/Low character data; neither equality is supplied as an input.

The constant-one convention is also proved directly for every actual row
whose relevant conductor parity is even.  This includes the Low base, norm,
twist, upstairs, and displayed-upstairs functions, and the High norm, twist,
upstairs, and displayed-upstairs functions.  Consequently the equality of
all High twist functions with the zero-index/base function is exported at

For the odd Low base conductor Lean proves the full displayed-upper
critical-function transport, not only its phase or its polynomial residual
class.  Put

 z=\operatorname{Fr}^{-1}(X),\qquad
 x=\varpi_K^d\,\iota([z]),\qquad
 P(y)=N(1+y)-1,\qquad H(y)=P(y)-\operatorname{Tr}(y),
\]
and let $u$ and $n=N(u)$ be the literal Low normalized ratio and norm.  The

 \Phi_K^{\mathrm{disp}}(X)=
 \Phi_{\chi_F}(X)\,\Phi_\tau(0)^{-1}

At the boundary it proves first, in the actual quotient at depth $d$, that
the norm polynomial has class

 [P(ux)]=\overline{N(u)}\,X,

and then proves

 \Phi_K^{\mathrm{disp}}(X)=

Here $\Phi_\tau$ is the actual index-one norm-row function at conductor
$t+1$ and depth $\lfloor(t+1)/2\rfloor$.  The inverse orientation is
literal.  The proof evaluates the actual stationary quotient
representatives, proves the upper and lower source reductions, and retains
the exact higher-norm correction; it does not invoke the residual coefficient

For the Low norm rows, the actual representative is the literal

It separately constructs the natural integer-multiple representative

character factor.  Thus the Teichm\"uller row is not silently identified
with the literal power row.

 c_{\tau\chi}=c_\chi+A_0\delta_h,

 z_0=\frac{N(n-u)}{n(1+n)},
 \qquad

 \operatorname{Err}_{K/F}(\chi_F)=H_2(D)\psi_F(-AX).

The $Q$-backed specialization derives $1+n\ne0$ from the high table's

The public result is a Type-valued structured record.  It contains the exact
High/Low dispatch, the supplied-representative API (including the stable high
compatible pair), the local Lamprecht API, the global four-factor error
decomposition and twist invariance, and the structured odd and quadratic

for the high and low odd results and the high and low quadratic complete

described, so an arbitrary independent rescaling of the upper, base, norm,
or twist residual functions is incompatible with that structured API.

representatives, and correction data.  Lean does not prove here the explicit
$\eta_0,d_0,\gamma_0,\gamma$ coefficient values of
\texttt{prop:odd-coefficient-table}, nor any downstream finite-field phase
cancellation.

Lean separately exports the canonical coordinate source and norm
compatibility, the canonical residue equivalence, and the quadratic,
representative, selected-coordinate, and residue-reindex transports.  A
structured residual-critical-function API is a proposition-valued proof
certificate about those fixed public definitions.  Its fields are only
theorems: applications of the named actual High and Low source-tied critical
functions, source-coordinate and representative transports, actual
stationary-ratio transports, the strict and boundary displayed-upper
identities above, quotient-class compatibilities, parity-complete actual-row
identities, and the two raw-row-ratio character bridges.  It has no function-, coordinate-,
coefficient-, or ratio-valued choice field.  In particular it cannot be
inhabited by replacing the fixed upper, base, norm, or twist functions by
independently rescaled alternatives.  This proof certificate is a field of
the public phase-reduction result, and its concrete proof is built from the
declarations above.
