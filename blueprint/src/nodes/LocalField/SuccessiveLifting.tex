\begin{theorem}[Successive filtered lifting]\label{mod:localfield-successivelifting}
Let $E$ and $F$ be nonarchimedean local fields, let
$\varphi:E\to F$ be a continuous monoid homomorphism, fix
$r\in\mathbf N$, and let $a:\mathbf N\to\mathbf N$ be monotone with
$a(n)\to\infty$.  Suppose that for every $n\in\mathbf N$ and every
$u\in U_F^{r+n}$ there is an $x\in U_E^{a(n)+1}$ such that
\[
  u/\varphi(x)\in U_F^{r+n+1}.
\]
Then for every $u\in U_F^r$ there is an $x\in U_E^{a(0)+1}$ with
$\varphi(x)=u$.

\SourceText{Proof of Theorem thm:norm-filtration, especially the complete lifting above the break.}
\uses{mod:localfield-lattices,mod:localfield-unitfiltration}
\FormalizationContract The proof chooses the corrections successively, proves that their
partial products are Cauchy from the valuation-lattice neighbourhood basis, takes their limit
using completeness of $E$, proves that the limit remains in $U_E^{a(0)+1}$, and uses continuity
of $\varphi$ to identify its image with $u$.
\end{theorem}
