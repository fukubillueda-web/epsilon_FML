\begin{lemma}[Stationary-class calculus]\label{mod:lamprecht-stationaryclasscalculus}
Let $F$ be a nonarchimedean local field.  Fix an additive character
$\psi$ of exact conductor $n$, an integer $M$, and
$\Gamma\in F^\times$ with
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_F(\Gamma)=M+n.
\]
All stationary numerators below are quotient classes; none of the
constructions selects a field-valued representative.

Suppose that $q>1$ and that $r$ is stationary, so
$q\leq2r$ and $r+1\leq q$.  If
$r\leq s$ and $s+1\leq q$, then $s$ is also stationary.  Replacing the
denominator ideal $\mathfrak p_F^{M-r}$ by
$\mathfrak p_F^{M-s}$ gives the representative-free projection
\[
 \operatorname{res}_{r,s}\colon
 \mathfrak p_F^{M-q}/\mathfrak p_F^{M-r}
 \longrightarrow
 \mathfrak p_F^{M-q}/\mathfrak p_F^{M-s}.
\]
It keeps the same numerator representative, and finite-duality uniqueness
proves
\[
 \operatorname{res}_{r,s}
   \bigl(\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r}(\theta)\bigr)
 =\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,s}(\theta).
\]

For $a\in F^\times$, put $v=\operatorname{ord}_F(a)$ and
$M'=M+v$.  Multiplication by $a$ descends, with its representative
independence proved at both bounds, to
\[
 \mathfrak p_F^{M-q}/\mathfrak p_F^{M-r}
 \longrightarrow
 \mathfrak p_F^{M'-q}/\mathfrak p_F^{M'-r},
 \qquad[c]\longmapsto[ac].
\]
Since $\operatorname{ord}_F(a\Gamma)=M'+n$, this map sends
$\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r}(\theta)$ to
$\operatorname{St}_{a\Gamma,r}(\theta)$.  Thus the arbitrary valuation of
$a$ is recorded by the shift from $M$ to $M'$; no unit assumption is hidden.

For a common layer $r\leq Q$, Lean's predicate
\texttt{IsStationaryRestrictionClass} says that a class
\[
 s\in\mathfrak p_F^{M-Q}/\mathfrak p_F^{M-r}
\]
linearizes a quasi-character on $\mathfrak p_F^r$.  It quantifies over every
$c$ representing $s$ and requires
\[
 \theta(1+x)=\psi(cx/\Gamma)
 \qquad(x\in\mathfrak p_F^r).
\]
The stationary class constructed in the preceding node satisfies this
predicate.  If $s_1,s_2$ linearize $\theta_1,\theta_2$ on the same layer,
then $s_1+s_2$ linearizes $\theta_1\theta_2$.  For every $j\in\mathbf Z$,
$j s_1$ linearizes $\theta_1^j$.  These are quotient-class statements;
their proofs descend arbitrary sum and integer-multiple representatives and
use the exact annihilator pairing to prove representative independence.

The minimal stationary depth is
\[
 r(q)=\left\lceil\frac q2\right\rceil.
\]
It is admissible for $q>1$, and if $q'<q$ then
$r(q')\leq r$ for every stationary depth $r$ at conductor $q$.
If $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$ both have exact conductor $q$, while their
product has exact conductor $q'$, then first
\[
 q'\leq q.
\]
Projection of the sum of stationary classes to the leading quotient
\[
 \mathfrak p_F^{M-q}/\mathfrak p_F^{M-q+1}
\]
vanishes exactly when the product conductor drops:
\[
 q'<q
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{lead}
 \bigl(\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r}(\theta_1)
       +\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r}(\theta_2)\bigr)=0.
\]
Equivalently, for every explicitly quantified pair of representatives
$c_1,c_2$ of the two stationary classes,
\[
 q'<q
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 c_1+c_2\in\mathfrak p_F^{M-q+1}.
\]
The proof uses exact triviality on $U_F^{q-1}$ and the exact left
annihilator $\mathfrak p_F^{M-q+1}$, not a pointwise-kernel converse.

Assume now that $1<q'<q$.  Form the product datum with its supplied exact
conductor $q'$, set $r'=r(q')$, and first construct the genuinely new class
\[
 \operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r'}(\theta_1\theta_2)
 \in
 \mathfrak p_F^{M-q'}/\mathfrak p_F^{M-r'}.
\]
Only then use the certified inclusions
\[
 \mathfrak p_F^{M-q'}\subseteq\mathfrak p_F^{M-q},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak p_F^{M-r'}\subseteq\mathfrak p_F^{M-r}
\]
to map this class into the old quotient.  There it equals the old sum:
\[
 \operatorname{res}_{(q',r'),(q,r)}
   \bigl(\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r'}(\theta_1\theta_2)\bigr)
 =\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r}(\theta_1)
  +\operatorname{St}_{\Gamma,r}(\theta_2).
\]
This two-bound map remains well typed even when $r\geq q'$.  In particular,
the construction never treats the old sum as a numerator class at the new
depth.  At the representative level, every $c'$ representing the newly
constructed class and every old pair $c_1,c_2$ satisfy exactly
\[
 c'-(c_1+c_2)\in\mathfrak p_F^{M-r}.
\]
If $q'\leq1$, no admissible stationary depth exists, so no stationary
parameter is retained and the conductor-zero or conductor-one endpoint
formula must be used.

\uses{mod:lamprecht-stationaryclass,mod:delta-elementary}
\FormalizationContract Every public stationary numerator is a certified
lattice-quotient class.  Restriction and scaling are representative-free
quotient maps.  Product and integer-power results quantify over all
representatives of a common-layer class.  After a conductor drop greater than
one, the class is constructed at the new conductor and new half-depth before
being compared with the old sum; at conductor zero or one there is no
stationary parameter.
\end{lemma}
